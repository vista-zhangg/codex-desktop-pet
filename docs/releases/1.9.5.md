# AgentPaw · AI 桌伴 1.9.5

1.9.5 改进行动中心的自动收起体验，让查看任务后回到桌面工作更顺手。

## 行动中心自动收起

- 点击桌面或其他应用、使桌宠窗口失去焦点时，行动中心自动收起；仅移出鼠标不会关闭面板。
- 从工作速览、额度或授权面板切换到行动中心时保留焦点，避免刚打开就被旧面板的失焦操作误收起。
- 收起面板不会处理或清除待办，重新打开后仍可继续查看；保留透明区域的点击穿透行为。

## 下载与升级

仅支持 **Windows x64**。从本页下载 **`AgentPaw-1.9.5-Windows-x64.exe`**，也可通过应用内更新升级。`latest.yml` 由应用更新器读取，无需手动下载。

## English

AgentPaw 1.9.5 closes the action center when the pet window loses focus after clicking the desktop or another application. Moving the pointer away alone keeps it open. Switching from the work peek, quota popover, or permission panel preserves focus so the action center stays open. Dismissing it leaves pending tasks intact and preserves click-through behavior in transparent areas.

Download `AgentPaw-1.9.5-Windows-x64.exe` or use the in-app updater. Windows x64 only; `latest.yml` is read by the updater.
